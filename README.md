# Ola Booking Analytics

End-to-end analysis of 100,000+ Ola booking records using Excel, SQL, Power BI, and DAX to identify booking trends, revenue patterns, cancellation behavior, and customer experience insights.

## Project Overview

This project analyzes Ola booking data to understand overall business performance and identify key operational and revenue insights.

### Business Questions

- What is the overall booking and success rate?
- Which vehicle types generate the highest booking value?
- What are the major sources of revenue?
- What are the main reasons for customer and driver cancellations?
- How do customer and driver ratings vary across vehicle types?

## Tools & Technologies

- **Google Sheets** — Data cleaning and duplicate removal
- **SQL** — Data querying and business analysis
- **Power BI** — Interactive dashboard and data visualization
- **DAX** — Measures and calculated metrics

## Dashboard Analysis

The Power BI dashboard contains five analysis sections:

1. **Overall Dashboard** — Booking volume, booking status, total booking value, and ride trends.
2. **Vehicle Type Analysis** — Booking value, successful booking value, and distance travelled across vehicle types.
3. **Revenue Analysis** — Revenue by payment method, customer contribution, and daily revenue trends.
4. **Cancellation Analysis** — Customer and driver cancellation patterns and cancellation rate.
5. **Ratings Analysis** — Customer and driver ratings across vehicle types.

## Key Insights

- **103,024 bookings** were recorded, with **63,967 successful bookings**.
- **Total booking value** was approximately **₹35M**.
- Successful bookings accounted for approximately **62%** of total bookings.
- **Prime Sedan** recorded the highest total booking value among the vehicle categories.
- **Cash and UPI** were the major payment methods contributing to booking value.
- Customer and driver cancellations represent a significant portion of unsuccessful bookings.
- Customer and driver ratings remained close to **4.0** across most vehicle categories.
